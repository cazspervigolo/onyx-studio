Meet the team

The team at Onyx.

Founder & owner · 7 years

Meet Renee

Hi, I’m Renee - founder and owner of Onyx. I’ve been barbering for 7 years and created the studio around quality service, strong attention to detail, and a space clients genuinely enjoy being in.

I’m experienced across all styles of men’s haircuts, from classic cuts to fades and modern styles, tailoring each service to suit the individual. I particularly specialise in scissor work, with a focus on shape, texture and movement - creating natural, well-balanced cuts that grow out nicely and feel effortless to wear.

My approach is relaxed but detail-driven - taking the time to understand what works best for each client, with good conversation, good energy, and a haircut that leaves you feeling confident and refreshed.

Senior barber · 10+ years · Valencia, Spain

Meet Mario

Hola, my name is Mario, and I’m originally from Valencia, Spain.

For me, hair design is much more than simply cutting hair. It’s about creating a style that complements each client’s head shape, hair texture, and lifestyle, while providing a unique experience that helps them feel confident and look their best.

With over 10 years of experience as a barber, I combine technical precision with a personalized consultation to create hairstyles that enhance each client’s individuality and natural features.
